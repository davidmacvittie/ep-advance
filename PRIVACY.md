# Privacy policy: EP Advance

Last updated: November 15, 2026

EP Advance is a free Claude plugin by David MacVittie. It holds two skills, EP Route Planner and EP Site Advance.

## What the skills use

- What you type into Claude: addresses, venue names, dates and times, and any names or notes you add.
- Public information that Claude looks up for you with its own tools: web search and fetch, and the map, traffic and weather connectors you have connected (such as Mapbox, TomTom and AccuWeather). Those services handle requests under their own privacy policies.

## What the skills do with it

- They build a PDF packet inside your Claude session. The skills ship no code. They tell Claude to write code that only draws the PDF in your session and makes no internet connections.
- Nothing is sent to David MacVittie or to any other service run by the author.
- Nothing is stored by the plugin. The packet stays in your Claude session and is yours to keep or delete.
- The skills do not read passwords, API keys or other credentials.
